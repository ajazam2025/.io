RESEARCH

Research at the intersection of hydraulics, rivers & data

My work combines experimental hydraulics, river-system studies, and machine learning to investigate flow and sediment processes in complex channels.

01
EXPERIMENTAL HYDRAULICS

Experimental & Mountain Hydraulics

Investigation of turbulence, flow dynamics, flow resistance and roughness in steep mountain channels, including channels with submerged boulders and complex bed configurations.

Sediment Transport & River Morphodynamics

Research on sediment transport, bedload prediction, bed shear stress, fluvial bedforms and river morphology, with particular interest in steep and alluvial channels and Himalayan river systems.
